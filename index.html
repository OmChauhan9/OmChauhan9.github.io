Editorial // Ethos

What's his deal?

01. Why focus on low latency and backend systems? ⚡
I dislike treating systems as black boxes. Whether it’s direct byte parsing in C++ to avoid heap allocation overhead or tuning queue-depth controllers for EC2 autoscaling, I care about mechanical sympathy: knowing what the hardware, kernel, and network are actually doing under load.
02. What do I look for in engineering teams? 🎯
High ownership, deterministic testing, and clear technical debate. I value teams that benchmark their assumptions, write reproducible integration tests, and prioritize truth and clean boundaries over corporate buzzwords.
03. Engineering non-negotiables 🛡️
No silent failures, strict P99 latency bounds, reproducible GoogleTest/JUnit test suites, and transparent tradeoff documentation. If an architecture makes a compromise, it should be measured and stated explicitly.
